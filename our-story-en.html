Our Story

Arbora was founded by Raymond and Daimy — a couple with a shared love for design, nature and meaningful craftsmanship. From their home and garden in Limburg, they began creating small wooden objects using reclaimed materials and honest tools.

What started as a creative hobby quickly grew into a way of life: restoring old furniture, designing timeless wooden objects and giving forgotten materials a new purpose. Every piece is made with care and tells a story of simplicity, sustainability and intention.

Arbora stands for more than design; it is about slowing down, choosing quality and staying close to what truly matters. Everything is handmade, locally produced and created with respect for nature and tradition.

Welcome to our journey.
