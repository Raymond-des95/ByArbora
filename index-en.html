Our craft

What is Arbora?

Arbora is derived from the Latin word for trees. A name that perfectly reflects our craft and our passion: creating unique products with wood at the heart of everything we do.

Based in Limburg, we design and create custom woodwork with care and craftsmanship, for both indoor and outdoor spaces. Wherever possible, we combine characterful reclaimed wood with high-quality new timber, giving every project its own distinctive look and story. Whether it is a walnut dining table, a stylish custom cabinet, a complete interior or a unique piece of furniture, every design is crafted with great attention to detail.

At Arbora, we believe wood is more than just a material. It brings warmth, character and atmosphere to every space. That is why we choose durable materials, high-quality finishes and solutions designed to last for many years.

Our possibilities are broad. From complete custom interiors and outdoor projects to tables, cabinets, lighting, planters and other unique wood creations. Do you have your own idea or a specific request? We would be happy to think along with you and turn your idea into a design that perfectly suits your home, garden or business.

Arbora stands for honest craftsmanship, natural materials and custom work made to last for generations.

Materials

Materials we work with

At By Arbora, every design begins with the material itself. Here you can discover the types of wood we work with, the finishes available, and the direction your own bespoke piece could take | inspiration, not a fixed catalogue.

Oak

Character Strong and robust, with a distinctive grain pattern that naturally stands out.
Suitable for Dining tables, built-in cabinets, bathroom furniture | anything that needs to be strong and remain beautiful for years to come.
Appearance Warm, natural tones ranging from light brown to golden brown, with a lively grain.
As with every type of wood, no two boards are the same. Growth rings, knots and variations in colour make every piece unique.

Walnut

Character Refined and luxurious, with a finer and calmer grain pattern than oak.
Suitable for High-end tables and designer furniture | pieces that are meant to make a statement.
Appearance Deep dark brown to chocolate tones, sometimes with a warm golden or subtle purple hue.
Colour variation within a single board can be significant | that is not a flaw, but part of what makes walnut so special.

Oak and walnut form the core of what we do, but depending on your project we are also happy to explore other types of wood | from robust Douglas fir to distinctive reclaimed timber. Just ask us about the possibilities.

Oak & finishing

The same wood, a completely different look

We work with Rubio Monocoat Oil Plus 2C | a wood oil that colours and protects in a single coat, with more than 40 colours available for oak and other wood species. The oil penetrates the wood and protects it from within, while changing the colour depending on the chosen shade | subtly or more dramatically. Here are a few examples, each shown on real oak so you get an honest impression of the result:

Pure No pigment, closest to the natural colour of the wood
Natural Subtle pigment, preserving a raw natural appearance
White 5% Light and airy, with a Scandinavian feel
Chocolate Warm and deep, with a rich wood tone
Walnut Deep brown, giving oak the appearance of walnut
Charcoal Bold and dark, with a contemporary contrast

These are 6 of the 40+ colours available from Rubio Monocoat for Oil Plus 2C | view the full range and order samples via rubiomonocoat.nl.

🌿 Wood is and always will be a natural product. The oil protects the surface, but regular maintenance | using the same Rubio Monocoat oil | remains important to keep your furniture looking beautiful and durable. We are happy to explain how this works when your piece is delivered.
