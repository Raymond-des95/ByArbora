Our craft

What is Arbora?

Arbora is derived from the Latin word for trees. A name that perfectly reflects our craft and our passion: creating unique products with wood at the heart of everything we do.

Based in Limburg, we design and create custom woodwork with care and craftsmanship, for both indoor and outdoor spaces. Wherever possible, we combine characterful reclaimed wood with high-quality new timber, giving every project its own distinctive look and story. Whether it is a walnut dining table, a stylish custom cabinet, a complete interior or a unique piece of furniture, every design is crafted with great attention to detail.

At Arbora, we believe wood is more than just a material. It brings warmth, character and atmosphere to every space. That is why we choose durable materials, high-quality finishes and solutions designed to last for many years.

Our possibilities are broad. From complete custom interiors and outdoor projects to tables, cabinets, lighting, planters and other unique wood creations. Do you have your own idea or a specific request? We would be happy to think along with you and turn your idea into a design that perfectly suits your home, garden or business.

Arbora stands for honest craftsmanship, natural materials and custom work made to last for generations.

Let's get started

From idea to unique result

Have a specific piece of furniture or a complete custom project in mind? At Arbora, we are happy to think along with you. From the first sketch to the final installation, we work together to create a design that fully matches your wishes, style and space.

We create custom cabinets, tables, TV units, outdoor furniture, complete interior and exterior solutions and other unique wood creations. Every project is carefully designed and crafted using high-quality materials, so you can enjoy a product that truly belongs in your space for years to come.

Because almost every project is custom-made, we do not operate like a traditional webshop. Personal contact, honest advice and craftsmanship are at the heart of what we do. Together, we discuss the possibilities and create a design that fits your wishes and budget.

Curious about the possibilities? Feel free to get in touch or schedule an appointment. We would be happy to discuss your ideas.
